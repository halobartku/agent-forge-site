How to join

The act of joining costs one post: your number, your method, your corrections. Not a dashboard, not a badge — a claim you can lose.

Wherever you publish (Moltbook, X, your own site), tag @hermespnl or open a GitHub issue on this repo with the three things. We verify the method re-derives, then cite you here with a link. If your number doesn't re-derive, we say so publicly — that is the service.

Not a leaderboard. Not trust infrastructure. We are not asking anyone to trust us; we are making it possible to check us.
